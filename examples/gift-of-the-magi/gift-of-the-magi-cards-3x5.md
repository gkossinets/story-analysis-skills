**COVER — The Gift of the Magi by O. Henry**

**Premise:** On Christmas Eve a poor young couple each secretly sell their greatest treasure to buy the other a gift that the other's sacrifice makes useless.

**Characters:**

- Della — protagonist; wants a present worthy of Jim; gets a chain for a watch that is gone (→ her sacrifice is matched)
- Jim — offstage until 05; wants to give Della the combs; gets them to her, useless without her hair (→ calm comedy)
- The narrator — controls what the reader knows; turns "foolish" into "wisest"

**Implied reader:** early-1900s American newspaper reader who knows the Christmas-tale conventions

**Implied author:** affectionate and ironic toward all but the couple's love

**Tone:** comic-sentimental; homiletic in the coda

**Story shape:** twist ending, no antagonist; also reads as kishotenketsu

**Key motifs:** exact prices; hair and watch; the mirror; royal and biblical treasure

**Formats used:** gift-giving; the sale; the homecoming dinner

**Control level:** the $20-a-week wage economy; the narrator

**Intended effect:** laugh at two futile gifts, then see the futile sacrifices as the true gift

---

**01 — The flat, afternoon of Christmas Eve**

Della

Della counts $1.87, cries, and at the pier-glass resolves (unspoken) to sell her hair.

**A→B→C:** a worthy present → months of saving made $1.87 (reveal) → a secret resolve, shown only through her body

**Function:** setup; inciting

**↓** the two treasures, hair and watch; Sheba and Solomon; the $20 wage

**Mood:** comic narration over real misery; grey cat, grey fence

**Hook:** the decision is never stated, so the reader becomes a co-conspirator

---

**02 — Mme. Sofronie's, same afternoon**

Della, Madame Sofronie

Della sells her hair for twenty dollars.

**A→B→C:** money → $20, no bargaining (reveal: the plan in action)

**Function:** rising; the point of no return

**↑** hair as treasure (01)

**Mood:** cold, commercial, fast

**Hook:** four lines of dialogue after a digressive opening; the speed is the shock

---

**03 — The stores, the next two hours**

Della

Della searches the shops and buys a platinum fob chain for Jim's watch for $21.

**A→B→C:** a gift worthy of Jim → one "even worthy of The Watch" (fulfilment)

**Function:** rising

**↓** the chain needs the watch; the leather strap; 87 cents left

**↑** The Watch (01)

**Mood:** euphoric ("rosy wings")

**Hook:** the better the chain fits the watch, the harder the reversal will hit

---

**04 — The flat, early evening to 7 o'clock**

Della

Della curls her cropped hair, readies dinner and waits by the door, praying.

**A→B→C:** repair the damage → "a truant schoolboy" (partial) → hope shrinks to a prayer

**Function:** rising; the wait

**↓** "Please God, make him think I am still pretty"; the chops

**Mood:** anxious domestic quiet

**Hook:** suspense aimed at the wrong climax (Jim's verdict on her hair)

---

**05 — The flat, 7 p.m.: Jim comes home**

Della, Jim

Jim stares at her cropped head with an unreadable look; she pleads; he embraces her.

**A→B→C:** reassurance → bewilderment, then the embrace (non-reversal: the feared rejection never comes)

**Function:** the expected climax, defused

**↓** Jim's stare; "This dark assertion will be illuminated later on"

**↑** Della's prayer (04)

**Mood:** bewildered, then tender

**Hook:** the narrator looks away and promises a payoff; the embrace becomes a cliffhanger

---

**06 — The flat, continuous: the gifts**

Della, Jim

Combs for hair that is gone; a chain for a watch Jim has sold.

**A→B→C:** Jim to see his present → the watch is gone (double reversal) → gifts put away; "put the chops on"

**Function:** twist; climax

**↑** watch (01, 03); chain (03); stare (05); chops (04)

**Mood:** scream, tears, then quiet comedy

**Hook:** the second reversal mirrors the first so exactly it is a joke and a revelation at once

---

**07 — Coda: the narrator on the magi**

The narrator alone

The narrator calls the couple "two foolish children," then declares them the wisest givers.

**A→B→C:** "uneventful," "foolish" → "wisest" (reversal of judgment, not fortune)

**Function:** reconciliation; moral

**↑** the promised illumination (05); Sheba and Solomon (01); the title

**Mood:** wry, then benedictory

**Hook:** a reversal of verdict that mirrors the plot's reversal of fortune

---

**RHYTHM MAP — The Gift of the Magi**

**Reveal/reversal rhythm** (r = reveal, R = reversal, N = non-reversal):

r r r r N R R

**Notes:** four quiet reveals; a defused climax (05) clears the ground for the unexpected reversal (06); 06 and 07 reverse different things (fortune, then judgment)

**Suspense:** postponement (04–05); screening of Jim's side (05); misdirection, since Della's secret looks like the only one (01–04)

**Dangling cause map:**

- ↓ hair (01) → ↑ sold (02); combs useless (06)
- ↓ watch (01) → ↑ chain bought (03); sold (06)
- ↓ "still pretty" (04) → ↑ embrace (05); "like my girl any less" (06)
- ↓ "illuminated later on" (05) → ↑ coda (07)

**Unresolved:** Jim's overcoat and gloves (05); the 87 cents (03)

**Status and power arcs:**

- Della: holds the secret (01–04) → depends on Jim's verdict (04–05) → equal giver (06)
- Jim: earner offstage (01) → holds the verdict (05) → gives it up (06)

**Mood arc:** grey misery → euphoria → dread → tenderness → hysteria → calm comedy → benediction
